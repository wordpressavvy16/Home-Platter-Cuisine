# Story 10.4.1: Refine Hanging Lamp Decoration (Iteration)

-   **Epic:** 10 (Theming & Personalization)
-   **Status:** Done
-   **Iterates on:** Story 10.4

---

### User Story:
As a user, during festive periods, I want the hanging lamp decoration to appear as a symmetric trio of lamps, with the central one hanging lower, to create a more grand and visually balanced festive display.

---

### Acceptance Criteria:

1.  **Given** a theme with the `hanging-lamp` decoration is active ("Diwali", "Navratri", "Ganesh Chaturthi").
2.  **When** a user views any page, **Then** three hanging lamps must be visible at the top-center of the screen.
3.  **Given** the three lamps are visible, **Then** they must be arranged symmetrically.
4.  **Given** the three lamps are visible, **Then** the middle lamp must be longer (hang lower) than the two side lamps.
5.  **Given** the three lamps are visible, **Then** all three must have the `gentle-sway` animation.

---

### Technical Specification:

-   **Component (`components/BackgroundDecorator.tsx`):**
    -   The rendering logic for the `sideDecoration: 'hanging-lamp'` has been updated.
    -   Instead of one lamp, it now renders three `HangingLampIcon` components wrapped in `div` containers.
    -   The central lamp container is positioned at the top-center and given a larger height.
    -   The two side lamp containers are positioned symmetrically on either side of the center lamp with a smaller height.
    -   Slight animation delays have been added to the side lamps for a more natural swaying effect.
