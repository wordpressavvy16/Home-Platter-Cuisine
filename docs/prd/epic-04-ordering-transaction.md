*   Secure, integrated payment gateway supporting multiple payment methods.
*   A transparent checkout process with clear breakdowns of costs and delivery estimates.
*   Real-time order tracking for customers.

#### 4.8 Business Rules & Constraints
- **Discount Exclusivity:** The Loyalty Points discount is mutually exclusive. It cannot be combined with any other promotional offer, including provider-set coupon codes or automatic volume discounts, within the same order.
