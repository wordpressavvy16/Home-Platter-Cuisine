### Epic 2: Provider Storefront & Product Management
*   A comprehensive dashboard for Providers.
*   Full CRUD (Create, Read, Update, Delete) functionality for their product listings (meals, pickles, spices, etc.).
*   Inventory management, order fulfillment tracking (e.g., 'Preparing', 'Shipped', 'Delivered'), and scheduling tools.
*   **Story 2.22 (Short-Form Video Integration):** A feature for providers to add a link to a promotional video (e.g., YouTube Short, Instagram Reel) that is prominently displayed on their public storefront, creating a more dynamic and engaging customer experience. (Done)
*   **Story 2.11 (Photo Gallery):** A feature for providers to upload and manage a photo gallery for their storefront. (Completed)
*   **Story 2.18 (Enhanced Caterer Profiles & Discovery):** A feature for providers to formally list catering services, including event types and guest capacity, and a dedicated discovery page for customers. (Completed)
*   **Story 2.19 (Refine Catering Feature Discovery):** A UI/UX refinement to improve the discoverability of the catering feature by replacing the header link with a prominent discovery card on the homepage. (Completed)
*   **Story 2.20 (Scalable Catering & Capacity Management Engine):** A robust system allowing providers to manage their catering availability. Includes a "Simple Mode" for small operators (max events per window) and a "Professional Mode" for larger businesses (max concurrent guests), with real-time availability checks on the customer inquiry form. (Completed)
*   **Story 2.21 (Provider Catering Quote & Customer Booking Confirmation):** A complete workflow for providers to send formal quotes in response to catering inquiries and for customers to accept and pay for these quotes, turning them into confirmed bookings. (Completed)
*   **Story 2.15 (Intelligent New Order Alert System):** A robust, schedule-aware audible and visual alert system for new provider orders, designed to grab attention in a busy environment while respecting the provider's defined work hours. (Completed)
