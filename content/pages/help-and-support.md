# Help & Support

Welcome to the Home Platter Help Center. We're here to assist you with any questions or issues you may have.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**How do I place an order?**
To place an order, browse the kitchens on our homepage, select a provider, add items from their menu to your cart, and proceed to checkout.

**How can I track my order?**
You can track the status of your current and past orders in the "My Profile" section of the app. You will receive notifications as your order is confirmed, prepared, and out for delivery.

**How do I become a provider?**
We're thrilled you're interested! You can sign up as a 'Kitchen Owner' from our main signup page. You will be guided through a setup process to create your storefront.
