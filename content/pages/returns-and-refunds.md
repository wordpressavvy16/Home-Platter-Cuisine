
# Returns and Refunds Policy

Due to the perishable nature of our products, we do not accept returns. However, we are committed to customer satisfaction.

If you are unsatisfied with your order for any reason, please contact the provider directly through the messaging feature on your order page within 2 hours of receiving your meal.

If you are unable to resolve the issue with the provider, you can contact Home Platter Support at **support@homeplatter.com** with your Order ID. We will review the case and may offer a refund or credit on a case-by-case basis.

**Issues that may be eligible for a refund:**

*   Wrong item delivered.
*   Order was not delivered.
*   Significant quality issues with the food.

We appreciate your understanding.
